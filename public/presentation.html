The Clinical Problem

Orthopedic surgeons currently make critical weight-bearing decisions blindly due to massive gaps in diagnostic toolsets.

X-Ray Limitations
X-Rays only show mineral deposits (calcium), not actual mechanical strength. A bone can regain full mechanical load-bearing capacity weeks before visual signs appear on X-Rays.
Subjective Decisions
Surgeons rely on qualitative feel, manual palpation, and static timeline calculations, leading to high variance in recovery rates and delayed returns to work.
Complications & Cost
10-15% of long-bone fractures suffer from delayed union or non-union. Detecting this late results in highly expensive secondary revision surgeries.

Target Niche: Tibia Fractures

We have prioritized the tibia as our primary clinical niche due to its unique anatomical characteristics and clinical high-stakes.

Anatomical Accessibility
The tibia is highly superficial (located directly under the skin along the shin). This minimizes acoustic attenuation from surrounding muscle tissue, enabling highly clean frequency sweeps.
High Rate of Non-Union
Because of limited blood supply in the distal third, the tibia has one of the highest rates of delayed and non-union fractures of any long bone in the human body.
Critical Load-Bearing Weight Decisions
As the primary load-bearing bone of the lower leg, deciding exactly when a patient can transition from crutches to full weight-bearing is crucial to prevent hardware failure.
Expandable Tech Stack
Solving for the tibia establishes a validated baseline framework that can be easily calibrated for other superficial bones (Clavicle, Ulna, Radius).

The ResoScan Solution

Non-invasive diagnostic device that listens to the structural resonance of the healing bone.

01

Direct Physical Measurement

Measures stiffness (Young's Modulus) directly via mechanical vibrations rather than looking at shadow-casting X-rays.

02

Zero Ionizing Radiation

Completely safe for frequent clinical scans, allowing bi-weekly tracking of healing progression.

03

AI-Powered Predictions

Proprietary AI models predict the exact day a patient can safely discard crutches based on dynamic stiffness index trendlines.

How It Works: Science & Physics

Structural frequency response shifts upward as fracture margins bridge and mechanical stiffness increases.

01
Controlled Impulse
A precise mechanical impulse is applied to the proximal end of the tibia (tibial tuberosity) using an automated low-frequency actuator sweep (20 – 1100 Hz).
02
Resonance Detection
A high-sensitivity sensor placed on the distal end (medial malleolus) records the transmitted signal, filtering out soft-tissue attenuation through custom DSP algorithms.
03
Peak Extraction
The resonance peak (fundamental natural frequency, f₀) shifts from ~300 Hz (fractured/loose) to ~800 Hz (healed/stiff), reflecting the bone's structural restoration.
